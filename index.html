Candle Care 101

The Candle Rituals

Follow these simple rituals to ensure a clean, long-lasting, and fragrant burn for your handcrafted candles.

1. Trim The Wick

Always trim your cotton wick to 1/4 inch (6mm) before every lighting to prevent excess soot and uneven burn.

2. The Memory Burn

Allow the wax to melt completely to the edges of the jar on your first burn (about 2 hours) to avoid tunneling.

3. Heat-Safe Surfaces

Place sculptural and floral candles on a ceramic dish or heat-resistant tray before lighting to catch melted wax.

4. Storage & Sun

Keep your botanical candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to preserve delicate wax pigments.
